<p style="text-align: justify;">
Yasmine Slimani is a Doctoral Researcher in Comparative Literature at the University of Bern. She holds an MA in English Literature from Bishop Grosseteste University, UK. Her doctoral thesis, Cultural Hybridity and Jewish Memory: A Comparative Analysis of Indian and Maghrebi Jewish Literatures, is supervised by Prof. Dr Axel Stähler (University of Bern) and Dr Rebekah Vince (Queen Mary University of London). She has presented her research at the Weidenfeld Institute of Jewish Studies at the University of Sussex and the Holocaust Research Institute at Royal Holloway, University of London.
